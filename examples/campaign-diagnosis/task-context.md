# Task Context — Diagnose Campaign Lead Quality

## Goal

- Desired outcome: improve qualified conversions without increasing the approved campaign budget.
- Checkable success definition: a qualified conversion is a submitted lead that meets all three current criteria—company has 20–500 employees, operates in an approved market, and requests a supported use case.
- Time period: diagnose the most recent 28 complete days against the previous 28 complete days.
- Audience or unit affected: leads generated by the selected paid campaign.

## Required Knowledge

- Business and offer: supported use cases, approved markets, sales capacity, budget ceiling.
- Customer and market: intended segment, common disqualifiers, evaluation language.
- Brand and communication: approved claims and current landing-page promise.
- Operating knowledge: where campaign, analytics, and CRM evidence live; approval boundaries.
- Evidence and performance: spend, clicks, form completions, qualified leads, source mapping, attribution caveats.
- Decisions and learning: previous audience, form, creative, and landing-page tests.

## Inputs and Tools

- Authoritative sources: approved campaign brief, ad platform, analytics property, CRM qualification record, decision log.
- Current external evidence: none required unless the diagnosis includes competitor or market change.
- Tools or connectors: read-only ad platform, analytics, CRM, and campaign files.
- Sensitive or restricted data: individual lead data must remain private and minimized.

## Work State

- Already completed: date range and campaign scope selected.
- Unresolved questions: CRM source mapping may be incomplete for a subset of leads.
- Known limitations: sales qualification delay means the last seven days are not mature.
- Next step: reconcile form completions with mature CRM qualification outcomes.

## Permission

- May research: yes.
- May draft: yes.
- May recommend: yes, with evidence and limitations.
- May prepare: proposed audience, creative, form, or landing-page changes for review.
- May act: no.
- Human approval required for: bids, budget, targeting, creative, forms, landing pages, CRM rules, or external communication.
