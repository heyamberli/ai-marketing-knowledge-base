# Example — Diagnose Campaign Lead Quality

This fictional example shows how a marketing agent can support a campaign diagnosis without receiving authority it cannot safely use.

## Scenario

A B2B campaign is generating more form completions, but sales reports that many leads do not meet the intended customer profile. The marketer asks an agent to improve qualified conversions without increasing the approved budget.

The agent may inspect, diagnose, and recommend. It may not change bids, audiences, creative, forms, or budget without approval.

## Why the Goal Comes First

Before receiving campaign history, the agent needs a checkable definition of a qualified conversion. Without it, more data may help the system optimize form volume rather than business value.

## Knowledge Used

- Business and offer: intended customer and commercial priority
- Customer and market: qualification characteristics and exclusions
- Brand and communication: approved claims and audience language
- Operating knowledge: permission and approval boundaries
- Evidence and performance: campaign metrics, CRM qualification, attribution limits
- Decisions and learning: prior tests and unresolved questions

The example is fictional. Its numbers and criteria are illustrative, not benchmarks.
