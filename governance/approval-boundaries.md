# Approval Boundaries

## Default Permission Levels

| Level | Agent may | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research | retrieve, organize, summarize, identify gaps | collect current campaign evidence |
| Draft | prepare content or a proposed action | draft three creative routes |
| Recommend | diagnose and propose a change with evidence | recommend pausing one audience test |
| Prepare | configure a reversible change for review | prepare but do not launch an ad revision |
| Act | change external state | publish, send, spend, launch, delete |

Default to research, drafting, and recommendation. Any permission to act must be explicit and task-specific.

## Success Definition Gate

Before increasing authority, confirm:

1. the desired outcome is written in a checkable form;
2. the agent can evaluate the relevant unit against that definition;
3. the necessary evidence is accessible and sufficiently reliable;
4. the action has appropriate brand, budget, privacy, and legal checks;
5. a human approval point is defined for material external action.

If success cannot be evaluated, permission collapses to analysis and drafting.
