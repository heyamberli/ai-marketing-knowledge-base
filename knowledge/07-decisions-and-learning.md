# 07 — Decisions and Learning

Use this domain to preserve what the program has decided, learned, changed, rejected, and superseded.

## Capture

- decisions and rationale;
- changed assumptions;
- successful and unsuccessful patterns;
- exceptions and contextual tradeoffs;
- rejected directions and why they were rejected;
- proposed learning awaiting validation;
- active and superseded guidance;
- effective dates and next review points.

## Questions

- What changed, and what evidence caused the change?
- Is the pattern repeated or still emerging?
- Which cases does the rule apply to, and where does it fail?
- Who approved the guidance?
- Did the learning improve later work?

## Current Knowledge

Do not silently promote an AI-generated interpretation into active guidance. Preserve the proposed state and the human decision.
