# 06 — Evidence and Performance

Use this domain to preserve what happened, how it was measured, and what the evidence can and cannot support.

## Capture

- primary sources and observation dates;
- metric definitions and measurement periods;
- campaign and content performance;
- experiments and test conditions;
- qualitative feedback and conversation evidence;
- proof points and approved claims;
- attribution, sample, freshness, and data-quality limits;
- conflicting evidence and alternative explanations.

## Questions

- What exactly was measured, during which period, and at what grain?
- Is this evidence, interpretation, or recommendation?
- What denominator or comparison is missing?
- Which confounders could explain the result?
- What evidence threshold would justify a durable change?

## Current Knowledge

Metrics without definitions, period, context, and limitations are incomplete inputs—not knowledge.
