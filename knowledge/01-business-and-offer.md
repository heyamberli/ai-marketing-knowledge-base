# 01 — Business and Offer

Use this domain to define what the organization does, what it can credibly promise, and which business outcome the work serves.

## Capture

- mission and business model;
- products, services, pricing, and availability;
- audience promise and value proposition;
- positioning and meaningful alternatives;
- current priorities and non-goals;
- commercial terms and commitments;
- offers the business is not ready or authorized to make.

## Questions

- What business outcome should this task improve?
- Which offer is current and available to this audience?
- What may we credibly promise?
- Which tradeoffs or exclusions must remain visible?
- What would count as optimizing for the wrong business outcome?
