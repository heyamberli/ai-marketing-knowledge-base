# 02 — Customer and Market

Use this domain to preserve what is known about the people, jobs, decisions, and environment the marketing serves.

## Capture

- customer segments and jobs to be done;
- problems, triggers, constraints, and objections;
- customer language and real questions;
- decision criteria and current workarounds;
- category structure, competitors, and alternatives;
- market changes relevant to the task;
- sample size, counterexamples, and evidence limits.

## Questions

- Who is the specific audience for this task?
- What are they trying to accomplish?
- What evidence supports the problem or segment assumption?
- What language do they use themselves?
- Is this a single signal, repeated pattern, or validated finding?

## Current Knowledge

Do not convert a generic persona or one conversation into a segment truth.
