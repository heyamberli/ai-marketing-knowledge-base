# 03 — Brand and Communication

Use this domain to define how the organization or person should be understood and represented.

## Capture

- message hierarchy;
- voice, tone, and vocabulary;
- channel-specific audience contracts;
- approved and rejected examples with reasons;
- public claim and evidence boundaries;
- visual identity and presentation rules;
- disclosure, privacy, and attribution requirements.

## Questions

- What is the one primary point this communication should make?
- Which language is accurate but wrong for this voice or audience?
- What evidence is required before making the claim?
- How should the framing change by channel without changing the underlying truth?
- Which examples best demonstrate the intended judgment?

## Current Knowledge

Save edit rationale when possible. A corrected sentence without the reason teaches less than the decision behind the edit.
