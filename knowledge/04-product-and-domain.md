# 04 — Product and Domain

Use this domain for product intelligence, category understanding, and the professional knowledge needed to judge the work.

## Capture

- product capabilities, limitations, and availability;
- use cases and workflow dependencies;
- tool-selection criteria and tradeoffs;
- current product state versus roadmap;
- observed behavior versus company claims;
- comparison logic and meaningful alternatives;
- domain frameworks and evaluation criteria.

## Questions

- What job does the product or method actually perform?
- What was directly tested, observed, or only claimed?
- What must be true in the user's workflow for it to be useful?
- What evidence would change the current verdict?
- Which domain expert should review a consequential conclusion?

## Current Knowledge

Do not present desk research, light testing, and sustained hands-on use as equivalent evidence.
